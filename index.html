Playbook 01
Architects

The trust gateway between BWM and the homeowner. 10 architects across Pune and Chandigarh validated that agencies cannot sell BWM directly - only an architect's specification carries the trust premium needed for homeowner acceptance. Time savings (1-day vs 7-14 day) is the universal pitch hook; warranty document and TDS are the procurement requirements.

Layer 1 From the universal six · pick these for architects
Time Saver - 1 day, not weeks
Universal #01

Every architect across both cities endorsed this framing as the strongest forward-pitchable USP. The Urban Grid (Pune): "That will be the best pitch we can get to a homeowner - ₹10 more expensive but tension-free, hassle-free, saves weeks." This is the headline architects will use with their clients.

10-Year Warranty - Backed by UltraTech
Universal #02

Architects with premium clients sign tripartite affidavits on stamp paper, accepting liability for 3-5 years. The Urban Grid demanded warranty "in writing." Pankaj Nanda requested TDS specifically for BOQ integration. UltraTech's manufacturer-backed warranty lets architects underwrite their own client obligations without bearing the risk personally.

International Standards - Specifier-grade certification
Universal #06

EN 1928, EN 12311-2, ANSI A118.12, EN 1328. The architect's specification language. Pankaj Nanda (Chandigarh) requested TDS specifically for BOQ integration: "Technical details are important for adding to BOQ and further processes." This is the procurement-language need the certifications + TDS together fulfill. AR_CHD_001 Aman did the same in the original dataset. Path to BOQ inclusion alongside Sika, Dr. Fixit, MYK, and Fosroc runs through these four standards.

Complete Home Envelope - One system. One warranty.
Universal #03

Eternal M. Creators (Pune) repositioned the product outright: "Best product for balconies and terraces, Strongest signal that the product name limits the pitch. Bathroom + balcony is universal across all 10 architects; roof requires explicit pitching.

Layer 2 Tailored only for architects
Structural Lift - thin profile, lighter slab, gained height
Tailored

Brick bat adds 75-100mm dead load on the slab and eats floor-to-ceiling height. BWM's thin profile is a structural advantage, not just a finish advantage. Eternal M. Creators: "Whenever we do brick bed or waterproofing, the slab load increases. We face height issues." Engineer-language USP - lands in the architect's professional vocabulary.

Beyond Bathrooms - the balcony + terrace bundle
Tailored

All 10 architects engaged on bathroom + balcony as the universal application pair. Eternal M. Creators repositioned BWM as "best for balconies and terraces Only

Procurement-ready deliverables - what to leave behind, every meeting
Tailored

The cutout-retention pattern: 5 of 5 architects who took the cutout committed. The procurement-deliverable pair: TDS (Pankaj Nanda's ask) + warranty document (Urban Grid's "in writing"). Every architect meeting leaves behind: cutout + TDS + sample warranty + editable Excel/Word BOQ template.

Playbook 02
Tilers

The applicator is physically present at the moment of tile-laying. One of the applicator architects use as their choice for BWM and the most easily-trained.

Layer 1 From the universal six · pick these for tilers
Time Saver - 1 day, not weeks
Universal #01

Your job finishes faster. You take more bathrooms per month at the same labour. T_PNE_005 Pawan flagged the curing-time coordination problem under traditional methods.

Simpler Protocol - 4 steps. One Applicator.
Universal #05

The official UltraTech video documents the exact sandwich method. You can master the protocol yourself, then cascade-train your in-house workers. T_CHD_001 in Chandigarh has flagged the same in-house ambition.

10-Year Warranty - Backed by UltraTech
Universal #02

Use this when closing premium customers - homeowners ask about warranty before they ask about price. 7 of 13 tilers in research already see warranty as an active sales tool. One of only two UltraTech products with a manufacturer warranty.

Layer 2 Tailored only for tilers
Income capture - you're already in the bathroom
Tailored

The membrane goes down with the adhesive that sits below the tile. Only one applicator is in the bathroom at that moment, and it is you. Adding BWM to your scope is not workflow disruption - it is the path of least resistance. Additional revenue per job.

You're the Pune architect's natural applicator pick
Tailored

Shree Brahma in Pune explicitly named the tile fitter as the BWM applicator. Eternal M. Creators uses the same civil agency for both tile and waterproofing - and you can be that agency. When BWM is architect-specified, the trade choice flows back to whoever the architect already trusts on that project. Build trust with one architect; volume follows.

Train once, monopolize the BWM trade in your area
Tailored

Two Chandigarh architects explicitly flagged applicator training as a friction point - Aakriti and The Creators. Whichever local tilers train first will lock in the architect-specified work in that city. First-mover advantage, ~2-3 day learning curve.

Playbook 03
Waterproofers

Caught in the cementitious/traditional methods.

Layer 1 From the universal six · pick these for waterproofers
Time Saver - 1 day, not weeks
Universal #01

Cleaner project handoffs, faster cycle through your project pipeline. WP_PNE_002 Anmol explicitly noted BWM lets the applicator skip the coat that traditional methods require.

International Standards - Specifier-grade certification
Universal #06

When you walk into an architect-led project, EN/ANSI compliance is the language that gets you specified. EN 1928 (watertightness), EN 12311-2 (tensile + elongation), ANSI A118.12 (crack isolation), EN 1328 (adhesion). WP_PNE_005 - the most BOQ-aware waterproofer in the dataset - confirmed architects' 4-brand list is built on standards-compliance.

Complete Home Envelope - One system. One warranty.
Universal #03

BWM uses the identical sandwich method on every surface - walls, floors, balconies, repair, new build - no cold-joint failure points. Particularly important for the floor-to-ceiling shower-wall coverage. WP_PNE_004 raised this gap directly during fieldwork.

Layer 2 Tailored only for waterproofers
Repair-easy - the segment's strongest endorsement
Tailored

6 of 7 waterproofers in research endorsed BWM as repair-viable - the strongest segment-level endorsement of any single use case in the entire dataset. Same customer, same problem, faster fix, manufacturer warranty in place of your personal guarantee.
